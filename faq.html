Basics

Should I rent a Private Island?

Yes. Rent a Private Island with an Airstrip and Pilot as soon as your travel income can cover it.

Why the Airstrip matters

  • The Airstrip and Pilot unlock private flights from the property.
  • Private flights are free, reduce travel time by 30%, and raise base travel capacity to 15 items.
  • Airstrip is a travel upgrade. It does not increase energy regeneration.

Why the PI itself matters

  • Property happiness boosts early gym gains. A fully upgraded PI with staff reaches very high maximum happiness.
  • Rent before buying. Owning a PI ties up billions that usually compound better in training, travel and bank interest early on.
  • Buy later when the purchase will not slow training, Donator upkeep, rehabs, bank deposits or travel trading.
Fast cash & reaching Level 15

Level 15 unlocks the economy

  • Use missions, safe wins and beginner-friendly crimes to push level 15 quickly. Level 15 unlocks travel.
  • Do not sit on full energy or nerve. Spend both every login while you build the account.
  • Balder's leveling guide is still a useful community reference, but check target safety before attacking.

Travel trading from Level 15

  • Current standard travel capacity is 5 items by default, 15 with PI Airstrip/Wind Lines Travel/Business Class, +4 from Large Suitcase, and up to +10 from faction Excursion.
  • Use live tools such as TornPDA, YATA or Torn Market prices before choosing a route. Plushie and flower margins move constantly.
  • Never carry more cash abroad than needed for stock. Bank, vault or faction-vault the rest before flying.

Layer faction income

  • Join Organized Crimes when assigned and supply requested tools or materials before the planning timer ends.
  • Chain when leadership calls it. Respect grows faction perks, and rewards are usually better when everyone participates.
  • Racket income depends on territory control and current racket level; do not plan around old fixed payouts.
Improving Organized Crime 2.0 success

What changed with OC 2.0

  • Organized Crimes now use scenario classes such as Lambda, Sigma, Phi, Psi and Omega, unlocked through faction core upgrades.
  • Leaders choose the scope, members plan for their assigned slots, and execution depends on the assigned team being available and ready.
  • Exact success math is not public, so treat hard stat thresholds as guidance, not guaranteed pass/fail rules.

How members help

  • Keep crime skill moving by doing Crimes 2.0 consistently and avoiding repeated failures on high-risk crimes.
  • Keep work stats growing through jobs, companies and education; many OC roles care about non-combat account development.
  • Be in Torn, out of hospital, not travelling, and above the required life threshold before execution.
  • Supply requested tools and materials early. If you cannot cover a slot, tell leadership before the timer becomes urgent.

Training & Stats

Efficient gym-gain routine

Daily cycle

  • Train before energy caps. Donator status raises the natural energy cap to 150 and improves daily energy flow.
  • Xanax gives 250 energy with a drug cooldown and addiction risk. Rehab before education, company or faction needs suffer.
  • Use the daily point refill when it is affordable; it is one of the cleanest repeatable training boosts.
  • Use PI happiness and short happiness boosts for early-stat sessions, then train immediately before happiness decays.

Compounding bonuses

  • Faction Steadfast can add up to +10% gym gains per stat, depending on faction upgrades.
  • Sports Science courses add smaller permanent gym bonuses by stat, plus a Bachelor bonus to all gym gains.
  • Unlock better gyms naturally by training. Do not over-optimize around a gym you cannot reach yet.
Stat training roadmap

Early account

Stay balanced enough to win chain targets and unlock gyms. Extreme one-stat builds can work later, but they make early account growth awkward.

Mid game

  • Keep all four battle stats useful unless leadership gives you a specific war role.
  • Strength and Speed win fights faster; Defense and Dexterity keep you alive. Neglecting either side creates easy losses.
  • Watch specialist gym requirements before pushing one pair too far ahead of the others.

Faction roles

  • Chain hitters need reliable wins and fast target turnover.
  • Wall defenders and ranked-war tanks can lean more defensive once the account is established.
  • Ask leadership before committing to a deep specialization.
Ready for the Chain? Everything you need to know

1. Mindset

Every member matters. If you can win clean fights, you can add respect. A small number of reliable hits can protect a milestone.

2. Current chain mechanics

  • A chain starts once the faction makes 10 valid attacks within five minutes.
  • After that, each valid hit resets the active chain timer to five minutes.
  • If the chain breaks, it enters cooldown at six seconds per hit made in the chain.
  • Bonus hits at chain milestones are where the biggest respect spikes happen. Follow leadership calls near 25, 50, 100 and larger milestones.

3. Respect quality

  • Respect is affected by target level, fair fight, war/retaliation bonuses, overseas hits, group attacks and attack type.
  • Do not farm low-quality targets during milestone pushes unless leadership asks for pure speed.
  • Losing gives no respect and can waste timing, so pick targets you can beat.

4. Gear & meds checklist

  • Carry enough medical items, blood bags if unlocked, ammunition and temporary weapons for the full window.
  • Stay in Torn, out of hospital and off flights unless leadership explicitly assigns a travel hit.
  • Use faction chat or Discord to avoid double-tapping targets and to coordinate end-push speed.

Progression & QoL

First stock perks to unlock

Use current stock-benefit names

  • WLT - Wind Lines Travel: private jet travel benefit and strong travel quality of life.
  • TCI - Torn City Investments: bank-interest bonus for long-term compounding.
  • FHG - Feathery Hotels Group: regular Feathery Hotel Coupons for training value or resale.
  • SYM / CBD / MCS / MUN: item dividends useful for drugs, nerve, energy and war supplies.
  • WSU / IST: education-focused perks if you are committing to long course queues.

Buying tips

  • Stock prices move. Compare the benefit value against bank interest, training spend and faction needs before buying.
  • Do not use obsolete names such as "Worldwide Stock Traders" or old flat gym-gain stock perks; those are not current stock benefit labels.
Is the Private Yacht worth it?

Not before Airstrip, Pilot and the core PI upgrades.

  • The current Property wiki lists Private Yacht as a high-value PI upgrade that adds 500 maximum happiness.
  • It is not the Airstrip and should not be treated as a travel-capacity upgrade.
  • Buy it only when the happiness value and prestige are worth the cash for your account. For early members, rent quality and training consistency matter more.
Key education courses

High-value early queues

  1. Sports Science: permanent gym-gain bonuses by stat and a Bachelor bonus to all gym gains.
  2. Biology: medical effectiveness, blood bags through Intravenous Therapy, and useful medical unlocks.
  3. Mathematics: Ammo Conservation and other combat-support utility.
  4. Combat Training: weapon accuracy and weapon-experience progression.
  5. Computer Science: hacking, cracking and several Crimes 2.0 support unlocks.

Time reduction

  • Education time reductions from merits, faction perks, principal jobs and stock benefits are additive up to the game cap for future courses.
  • Keep a course queued at all times. Empty education time is permanent progress lost.

Warfare & Advanced

Budget combat load-out

Starter priorities

  • Use weapons you can actually hit with. Accuracy and consistency beat expensive gear you cannot use well.
  • Carry a primary, secondary, melee weapon, armor and temporary weapons. Keep ammo stocked before chains or wars.
  • Bring medical items and the correct blood type once Intravenous Therapy is unlocked.

Upgrade path

  • Improve armor before buying luxury weapons if you are still getting farmed.
  • Use weapon mods when they fit your accuracy, ammo and role. Do not spend rare resources without asking if you are unsure.
  • For ranked war, follow leadership target calls and do not burn expensive supplies outside the assigned window.
High-value territory rackets

Current high-value racket types to watch:

  • Illegal Casino: high cash output at top level.
  • Point Broker: daily points at higher levels.
  • Money Launderer / Protection Racket: strong cash rackets when leveled.
  • Truck Stop: energy-can output that can support chain and training stock.
  • Drug Lab / Street Surgeon: useful supply generation for drugs and medical items.

Racket value depends on level, location and current map pressure. Central or contested blocks can be worth defending even when the current payout looks ordinary.

Extras

Growing your nerve bar

Crime 2.0 loop

  • Your natural nerve bar grows from long-term crime progression. Successes matter; repeated failures slow you down.
  • Crime Chain gives extra crime experience and crime skill as it grows. A normal failure halves the chain, while a critical failure resets it.
  • Push new crimes slowly. Stay on safer outcomes until success rates are stable, then move up.

Nerve sources

  • Nerve naturally regenerates over time and can be supplemented with alcohol, cannabis, LSD, point refills and certain stock or faction benefits.
  • Faction Criminality can increase maximum nerve and improve crime progress depending on active upgrades.
  • Use nerve before flying or logging off for long stretches.
High-efficiency daily checklist
  • Energy: train before cap, use Xanax/refill only when you can train safely after cooldowns.
  • Nerve: spend it on Crimes 2.0 before long idle windows.
  • Travel: check live stock, buy efficiently, and avoid landing with full energy or nerve.
  • Faction: fill OC slots, answer war/chain calls, and keep supplies ready.
  • Education: keep the next course queued before the current one ends.
  • Money: keep cash protected through bank, vault, stocks or faction systems as appropriate.
Is Donator status worth it?

Benefits

  • Energy cap rises from 100 to 150.
  • Energy regeneration improves from 5 energy every 15 minutes to 5 energy every 10 minutes.
  • That is 720 natural energy per day instead of 480, before Xanax, refills, cans or other boosts.

Funding

  • A Donator Pack gives 31 days of Donator status and points when used.
  • A monthly subscription gives 31 days of status plus subscription rewards. Compare real-money cost against market price if buying with Torn cash.

For an active player, Donator is one of the highest-impact account upgrades because energy compounds into battle stats every day.
