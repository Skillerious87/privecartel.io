The standard

Relaxed does not mean absent.

We do not manufacture pressure. We do value people who stay connected, protect the faction’s momentum and communicate when real life takes priority.

You will fit well here if you are self-directed, considerate and comfortable contributing without needing to be chased.

    Live our three principles: presence, discretion and mutual aid.

Before you apply

The useful details, upfront.

Good recruitment starts with clarity. These are the questions applicants usually need answered first.

Are wars mandatory?

No. Privé Cartel does not force members into wars. We ask for sensible participation in chains and faction activity when you are available.

How active do I need to be?

Our standard rhythm is at least one login every 24 hours when real life allows. If you need time away, communicate with leadership so we can plan.

What should I include in my application?

Your Torn name, level, activity pattern, current goals and availability for OC 2.0 or chains are enough to start a useful conversation.

Is Private Island access guaranteed?

No. The two-week arrangement is for eligible newer members and depends on the current property rotation and availability.
