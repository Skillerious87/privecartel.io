Availability and response

Activity Expectations

Daily Presence
Log in at least once every 24 hours. Last Action > 24 hours flags you as inactive.
Event Notifications
Enable push alerts in the Torn mobile app, or email/SMS via Settings → Preferences, so OC and chain pings reach you quickly.
OC Commitment
If you join an OC 2.0 scenario, remain active for its full 3-6 day timer and the sub-1-minute execution phase. Missing execution voids the run and burns Scope.
Chain Windows
Only members who opt in must respond in under 5 minutes during scheduled chain windows. Keep 50+ Energy banked, fast targets bookmarked and hospital time under 10 minutes.
Vacation Mode
Away for 72+ hours? Toggle Vacation Mode in Torn and drop an ETA in faction chat. Slots are protected; no notice can mean removal after 3 idle days.
Quick Response
Leaders may send urgent in-game mail or Discord pings. Reply when possible so rosters can adjust in real time.

Faction operations

Organised Crimes 2.0

Scenario System
Every OC is a named scenario from level 1-9. Difficulty tiers are Lambda, Sigma, Psi and Omega, with Scope costs scaling by tier.
Scope Pool
Factions start with 25 Scope, cap at 100 and regenerate +1 Scope per day. Completing scenarios can also award bonus Scope.
Planning to Execution
After the day-countdown, a sub-1-minute execution fires. All members must be in Torn, healthy and carrying required items or the run can fail.
Roles and Items
Participants fill role slots based on suitability and success score. Materials such as C-4 and zip-ties are consumed; tools such as Jemmy and DSLR persist.
New Member Guide
Before joining your first scenario, read the Organized Crimes 2.0 guide for slot selection, item checks, timers and payout expectations.
Payout Split
On success, leadership chooses the split between faction and members. Cash can hit wallets or the faction bank; item loot is distributed randomly or sold at market value.
Readiness
Join only if you can stay active for the full 3-6 days and keep Last Action under 24 hours. If you cannot log in, ping leadership immediately.
Mandatory OC participation

Any member who does not join an OC within seven days will receive a formal warning. Failure to participate within fourteen days can result in removal. Real-life commitments are fine; notify leadership early.

3

Reputation and diplomacy

External Conduct

Your actions outside the faction reflect on all of us. Follow these rules when dealing with other factions, markets and public channels.

Faction Relations Tags
Leaders control our status with other factions. Do not provoke, negotiate or promise favours on your own.
Target Clearance
Obtain approval before hospital sprees, territory raids, high-value bounties or chain hits on ranked allies. One rogue attack can invite serious retaliation.
No Outside Help
Arranging mercenaries or intimidation squads without leadership consent is forbidden. Recruiting outsiders, or threatening to, counts as hostile action.
Trade and Marketplace
Use Torn's Trade window or Item Market. Screenshot deals above $50m. No real-money, cross-game or crypto trades.
Chat, Forums and Global
No slurs, doxxing or real-life threats. Keep internal strategy off public channels and mark jokes clearly to avoid misfires.
Harassment
Light banter is fine. Stalking, spam PMs and strong-arm tactics are not. Torn logs messages and reviews complaints.
Scamming
The faction permits buy-mugging as a gameplay mechanic. Any transaction or agreement, internal or external, is binding and must be honoured.
Zero tolerance

Members who act externally without consulting leadership can be expelled swiftly.

How we operate internally

Internal Culture

We build each other up through guidance, generosity and shared knowledge. Lean in, ask questions and give back.

Mentoring
New arrivals can request a mentor for builds, routes and revenue strategies.
Resource Pool
Donate spare meds, ammo or job points to the armoury. What helps one member can help the whole faction.
Knowledge Sharing
Post patch-note finds, economy tips and smart-play advice so the entire faction stays ahead.
