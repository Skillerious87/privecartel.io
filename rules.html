Charter foundation

Core Tenets

These principles underpin every operational rule. Honour them, and the Cartel will stand firmly behind you.

Presence
Be present when it matters. Consistent activity keeps the faction dependable.
Discretion
Keep faction business private. Sharing internal messages, screenshots or strategy may result in removal.
Mutual Aid
Support fellow members when you can, and be willing to ask for help when you need it.
1

Availability and response

Activity Expectations

Consistent activity keeps the faction dependable. Stay visible, respond when needed and let leadership know before real life takes you away.

Daily Presence
Log in at least once every 24 hours. If your Last Action exceeds 24 hours without an approved absence, leadership may remove you from the faction.
Event Notifications
Enable the Torn and Discord notification channels you rely on so OC, chain and leadership pings reach you promptly.
OC Commitment
If you join an OC 2.0 scenario, remain available throughout its displayed planning window. Be healthy, in Torn and ready with every required item before initiation.
Chain Windows
Members who opt in must respond within five minutes during scheduled chain windows. Keep at least 50 Energy banked, fast targets bookmarked and hospital time below ten minutes.
Planned Absence
If you expect to be away for 24 hours or longer, privately message a leader through Torn or Discord before leaving and include your expected return. Faction-chat posts do not count as notice because they can quickly be buried. Use Vacation Mode for longer breaks; approved absences will not be treated as unexplained inactivity.
Quick Response
Leaders may send urgent in-game mail or Discord pings. Reply when possible so rosters can adjust in real time.
Membership standard 24-hour inactivity policy

More than 24 hours of unexplained inactivity may result in removal from the faction. To protect your place, send a leader a private Torn message or Discord DM before you leave. A message in faction chat is not accepted as notice.

2

Faction operations

Organised Crimes 2.0

Organised Crimes require preparation and dependable participation. Confirm that you can meet the full commitment before taking a role.

Scenario System
Every OC is a named scenario from levels 1–10. Difficulty tiers are Lambda, Sigma, Phi, Psi and Omega, with Scope costs increasing by tier.
Scope Pool
Factions start with 25 Scope, can hold up to 100 and regenerate one Scope per day. Completing scenarios may also award bonus Scope.
Planning to Execution
When planning completes, initiation lasts less than one minute. During that phase every participant must remain healthy and in Torn, with all required tools and materials available, or the run may fail.
Roles and Items
Participants fill role slots based on suitability and success score. Materials such as C-4 and zip-ties are consumed; tools such as Jemmy and DSLR persist.
New Member Guide
Before joining your first scenario, read the Organized Crimes 2.0 guide for slot selection, item checks, timers and payout expectations.
Payout Split
On success, leadership chooses the split between the faction and participants. Cash can go to wallets or faction balances; item rewards may be allocated randomly or paid out at market value from faction funds.
Readiness
Join only if you can remain active for the scenario's displayed planning time and keep your Last Action time below 24 hours. If your availability changes, notify leadership immediately.
Participation requirement Mandatory OC participation

Members who do not join an OC within seven days will receive a formal warning. Failure to participate within fourteen days may result in removal. Real-life commitments come first; notify leadership as early as possible.

3

Useful, welcoming communication

Faction Chat Standard

Faction chat is our shared operations room. Faction business takes priority, with room for friendly off-topic conversation when it does not crowd out coordination or make others uncomfortable.

Business Comes First

Keep OC coordination, chain calls, faction alerts, member help and leadership messages visible. Pause or move casual conversation when operational messages need attention.

Keep It Civil

Disagree without insults, baiting, harassment, slurs or dogpiling. Banter is welcome only while everyone involved is comfortable; stop when asked.

No Promotion or Soliciting

Do not advertise other factions, servers, services, referral links, streams or personal projects, and do not recruit members, unless leadership has approved it first.

Keep It Appropriate

Sexual or explicitly suggestive conversation, sexual harassment, and NSFW images or links are not permitted. Keep the chat comfortable for a broad member audience.

Light off-topic chat is fine. Move long conversations, screenshots, voice chat and anything that starts dominating the channel to Discord.

4

Reputation and diplomacy

External Conduct

Your conduct outside the faction reflects on every member. Follow these standards when dealing with other factions, using the market or posting in public channels.

Faction Relations
Leadership manages our relationships with other factions. Do not provoke disputes, negotiate agreements or promise favours on the faction's behalf without approval.
Target Clearance
Obtain approval before hospital sprees, territory raids, high-value bounties or chain hits against ranked allies. One unauthorised attack can invite serious retaliation.
Outside Intervention
Arranging mercenaries or intimidation squads without leadership consent is forbidden. Recruiting outsiders, or threatening to, counts as hostile action.
Trade and Marketplace
Use Torn's Trade window or Item Market, and keep evidence of deals worth more than $50m. Real-money, cross-game and cryptocurrency trades are prohibited.
Public Channels
Slurs, doxxing and real-life threats are prohibited. Keep internal messages, screenshots and strategy out of forums, global chat and other public channels.
Harassment
Light-hearted banter is welcome; stalking, repeated unwanted messages and intimidation are not. Save relevant evidence and report concerns privately to leadership.
Scamming
The faction permits buy-mugging as a gameplay mechanic. Any transaction or agreement, internal or external, is binding and must be honoured.
Enforcement notice Protect the faction

Any external action that could affect the faction's reputation, diplomacy or security must be cleared with leadership first. Serious or repeated breaches may result in immediate removal.

5

How we operate internally

Internal Culture

We build each other up through guidance, generosity and shared knowledge. Lean in, ask questions and give back.

Mentoring
New arrivals can request a mentor for builds, routes and revenue strategies.
Resource Pool
Donate spare medical items, weapons, armour or other useful supplies when leadership requests them. What helps one member can help the whole faction.
Knowledge Sharing
Post patch-note finds, economy tips and smart-play advice so the entire faction stays ahead.
6

Risk management

Conflict & Warfare

Avoid unnecessary conflict and protect the faction's energy, respect and perks. Only take part in coordinated action when leadership calls for it.

Non-Warring Stance
We prioritise profit and preservation over conquest.
Energy Drain
Random hits waste energy better spent on training, chains and coordinated faction events.
Perk Protection
Sustained losses can threaten respect-funded upgrades, member capacity and faction benefits.
Defensive Hits
When leaders call a shield wall, one timely revive or defend can help end hostilities.

Live coordination

Private Lounge

Use faction chat for live business and light conversation. For longer strategy discussions, screenshots, voice chat or extended off-topic conversation, use the Discord lounge.
