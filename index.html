The Cartel code

A quieter kind
of power.

In Torn’s chaos, Privé Cartel is the room behind the room—where progress is deliberate, respect is non-negotiable and every member strengthens the whole.

  1. Presence

    Stay connected, confirm activity and be ready when the faction moves.

  2. Discretion

    Internal business stays behind the curtain. Trust is earned and protected.

  3. Mutual Aid

    Knowledge, resources and support move freely when another member calls.

Live by the standard. The Cartel stands behind you.Respect the room, protect its trust and support the people beside you.

Built through every era · Est. 2024

Four names.
One loyalty.

Privé Cartel is the current incarnation of a crew shaped by ranked wars, disciplined chains and organised crime. The name changed as the faction matured; the loyalty underneath it did not.

  1. Feb–Apr 2024

    War Fanatics

    A scrappy brawl school forged through short chains and an upset ranked-war win that carried the crew into Bronze I.

  2. Apr–Oct 2024

    WF Academy

    ForeverHydrox reshaped the faction into a training ground, with disciplined chain work and a stronger leadership core.

  3. Oct 2024–May 2025

    PB Enterprises

    Bigger ambitions brought the first 100-hit chain, deeper resources and an early move into Organised Crimes 2.0.

  4. May 2025–Present

    Privé Cartel

    A quieter, sharper identity: sustained triple-digit chains, deliberate operations and a culture built to last.

One crew. Four eras. One shared standard.Presence, discretion and mutual aid remain the foundation beneath every operation.
